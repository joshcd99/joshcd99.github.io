Why should we hire you?

I don't just write software. I build the operational nervous system for industries that still run on fax machines and spreadsheets. As Operations & Technology Lead at Great Lakes Radiant & Industrials, I designed and built an integrated shipping platform that compares LTL freight rates across three brokers in real time, books shipments, writes carrier and cost data back to NetSuite via SuiteScript, and sends branded tracking emails to customers. The accompanying rate-comparison analysis (400 lanes across four shipping origins) is projected to save the company about $16,900 a year and is driving an active vendor negotiation. I know how a sales order moves from a customer quote through ERP to a shipping label on a pallet, and I know what a radiant heating loop actually is. Most CS grads can build CRUD apps; very few can sit with a sales engineer, walk a warehouse floor, and then write the middleware that connects all of it.

I also have the range: at Punch Through I designed an OTA firmware-update workflow for BLE devices in Swift, and shipped SwiftUI/CoreBluetooth features in LightBlue (120,000+ MAUs). At TechSmith I built macOS Snagit features in Swift/Objective-C with SwiftUI/AppKit and led the background-noise-removal UI, delivered ahead of schedule with 95% positive on user surveys.

My liberal-arts angle: a St. Thomas education pushed me toward human-centered design thinking (literal HCI coursework) that I apply to unglamorous internal tools and to consumer software alike. I've also spent 6+ years as a FIRST Robotics technical mentor and currently volunteer with Big Brothers Big Sisters, which is to say: I care about teaching, about people, and about building things that hold up when handed to someone else.

I not only have full-stack engineering and systems-integration skills, but also operational empathy, embedded/mobile range, and a sustained commitment to mentorship. The combination is what lets me build software people actually adopt.
